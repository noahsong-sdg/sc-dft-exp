# Figure provenance and interpretation

Generated by `research/plot_response.py` using matplotlib 3.9.4/numpy 2.0.2.
Run `.venv/bin/python research/plot_response.py` from repository root after the
bounded retrieval. Source URLs/hashes are in ../retrieval_manifest.json.

Underlying data: Kawamura, Nomoto, Witt and Arita's public V3Si release,
https://doi.org/10.1103/lrhl-j9bt , linked at https://mkawamura.ynu.ac.jp/data/ .
These are new plots/diagnostics, not copied publication figures.

released_response: left uses the archived plot's conversion of reported current
to A/cm²; right compares origin-constrained fits over the first 1–4 nonzero
samples, colors shared with the left legend. Those windows may include physical
nonlinearity. Their spread is not an error bar or proof of the Q->0 limit. The
released zero-Q point is processed as zero; raw code's projected current is
undefined there. No corrected material current is shown.

extraction_routes: reproduces archived script+plot conversions for both methods.
The 1 K slope is 97.60 nm, close to the published 97 nm; peak route is 177.09 nm
versus published 136 nm, still unresolved. Sampled maxima and nearest half-gap
points are used exactly as in the archived script, without new interpolation.
This is a reproduction diagnostic, not independent experimental validation.

Both PDF vector and PNG raster versions are provided. Axes/legends were visually
inspected on the response figure; they render without clipping at output size.

## Fixed-input Nb reduced/full validation

`gap_validation.png` and `.pdf` are generated by
`.venv/bin/python research/plot_gap_validation.py`. The script reads only committed
JSON diagnostics; `gap_validation_sources.json` records their SHA256 hashes.
The left panel is a reduced coefficient residual, not the full gap residual.
The middle panel shows candidate sensitivity to enrichment, not experimental
gap measurements. The right panel divides independently measured full native
residuals by their registered targets; both ratios must be ≤1. Rank38 passes
both stationary-residual targets. Rank32 was not full-validated and receives no
invented full-space point. Ranks31/33/34/35 fail both;36/37passRMS but failmaximum.
The rank38 PNG was visually inspected for scales, labels and clipping. Connecting
lines only guide the eye. These retained fixed inputs do not establish fresh
basis setup, discretization convergence, conditioned gap error or physical
response accuracy.

## Anisotropic candidate response

`plot_anisotropic_response.py` generates `anisotropic_response.png`/`.pdf` from
`anisotropic_candidate_response_series.json`; its hash is in
`anisotropic_response_sources.json`. Left: second-moment response on retained
resolved support plus the unchanged native single-state contribution. Right:
computed absolute moment-averaging bound divided by that combined response.
Bounds concern this numerical operation at each unmodified candidate. They do
not bound self-consistent gap error, discarded support, discretization, current
functional accuracy, or material prediction error. Rank38 separately meets
stationary-residual targets; no candidate is a validated physical prediction.
The updated rank38 PNG was visually inspected for scales, labels and clipping. The full48-operation geometry deliberately exercises the documented
absolute-selector defect; it is not the qualified directional small-Q method.
See ../ANISOTROPIC_MOMENT_GATE.md for assumptions and floating-point scope.


## Electronic-grid and velocity-stencil control

`grid_stencil.png`/`.pdf` come from `.venv/bin/python research/plot_grid_stencil.py`.
The script verifies the comparison's frozen source hashes before rendering;
`grid_stencil_sources.json` binds the plot script, comparison and original
result artifacts. Left: the2K conserved diagnostic normalized to fourth-order48,
so values are dimensionless rather than penetration depth. Right: observed32→48
percentage change at2/4/8K, second versus fourth velocity stencil. The spread
reduces from about5.52% to0.68%. Connecting lines guide the eye; there are no
statistical error bars or continuum-error bounds. The gap is imposed1.5meV,
density and512response cells fixed; full-symmetry tiny-Q remains a diagnostic.
The PNG was visually inspected for labels, scales and clipping.
