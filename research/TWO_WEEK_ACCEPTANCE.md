# Active scientific acceptance gates

Objective: reliable superconducting response calculations on this laptop, with
public progress. This is not closed by the previous bounded diagnostic audit.

## Prospective material case

Primary observable: the static, transverse long-wavelength screening stiffness
and penetration depth of clean, nonmagnetic bulk niobium, including the normalized
temperature dependence. Initial temperature targets are 2, 4 and 8.05 K. The
retained 8.05 K stationary problem is a reproduction anchor, not an authorized
substitute for the other temperatures or their Z/kernel contexts.

The scientific decision is whether a finite-temperature anisotropic gap changes
the normalized response enough to distinguish it from a justified isotropic or
constant-gap approximation using the same microscopic inputs. Report a physical
length only after current normalization and functional assumptions are qualified.
This is an intended beneficiary/use case for method/material researchers, not
verified demand or novelty.

Prospective numerical target: at most 1% relative numerical uncertainty in each
accepted stiffness and normalized ratio, with separately allocated solver,
conditioning, energy/electronic/response-grid, interpolation and derivative
contributions. This is a chosen resolution target, not an achieved bound. An
observed grid difference alone is insufficient. A claimed distinction must exceed
the combined justified uncertainties; a null distinction is allowed. Experimental
sample/model discrepancy is separate and must not be absorbed by fitting a factor.
